Cookie Policy

What this website does — and doesn't — store in your browser.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Our current cookie use, in short

This website does not currently set tracking or advertising cookies, and does not use analytics software that identifies individual visitors. We've kept it this way deliberately to keep things simple and privacy-respecting.

What does load when you visit

  • Google Fonts — this site loads typefaces from Google's font service (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may log basic technical request data (such as your IP address) as part of serving these fonts, under Google's own privacy policy. No cookies are set by this on our end.
  • Contact form (Formspree) — when you submit the contact form, your entry is sent to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, so it can reach our inbox. This happens only if you actively submit the form.
  • WhatsApp — the WhatsApp button on this site links out to WhatsApp's own website or app, which has its own separate cookie and privacy practices.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising, or other tracking technology in the future (for example, to understand how visitors use the site), we'll update this page to explain what's used and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, reach out to rolvadigitaltechnologies@gmail.com.
