About us

Built by an engineer who tests for a living

Rolva Digital Technologies (Pty) Ltd is a South African quality engineering consultancy, founded to bring enterprise-grade test automation practices to teams that need to ship reliably — not just quickly.

The practice is led by a test automation engineer and QA specialist with hands-on experience building regression and payment-testing suites inside a major banking group, now applied to clients directly: real frameworks, wired into real pipelines, owned by your team when we're done.

Quality engineering, end to end

Test automation frameworks

Quality strategy & consulting
